Centre for Language Sciences

The Centre

Welcome to the Centre for Language Sciences (CLS) at IIT Kanpur.

The Centre is a multidisciplinary research and development programme to advance Language Science and enable its applications in health, education, technology, and culture.

The Centre brings together researchers from multiple disciplines, including Cognitive Science, Linguistics, Computer Science, and Philosophy, to undertake experimental, computational, and theoretical research on language.

Its research seeks to understand how language is processed in the mind, how languages evolve and differ, how they converge, and how they are distributed structurally.
