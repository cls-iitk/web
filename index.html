Welcome

Welcome to the Centre for Language Sciences (CLS) at IIT Kanpur! The CLS is a multidisciplinary research and development program to advance Language Science for Indian languages and enable its applications in health, education, technology, and culture. The Centre was established with funding from the Anusandhan National Research Foundation (ANRF) under its flagship Convergence Research Centres of Excellence (CoEs) program.

The Centre aims to undertake extensive experimental, computational, and theoretical research on Indian languages (ILs) to understand how ILs are processed in the mind, how they evolve, how they differ, how they converge, and how they are distributed structurally, and use these research insights to develop solutions for assessment of language proficiency, diagnosis and treatment of language disorders, second language learning and education, mental health and emotional assessment, and NLP and AI in low-resource Indian languages.

The Centre is led by a group of researchers from multiple disciplines (Cognitive Science, Linguistics, Computer Science, and Philosophy) at IIT Kanpur.
