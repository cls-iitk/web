THE CENTRE

Centre for Indian Language Sciences

Welcome to the Centre for Indian Language Sciences (CILS) at IIT Kanpur!

The CILS is a multidisciplinary research and development program to advance Language Science for Indian languages and enable its applications in health, education, technology, and culture. The Centre was established with funding from the Anusandhan National Research Foundation (ANRF) under its flagship Convergence Research Centres of Excellence (CoEs) program.

The Centre aims to undertake extensive experimental, computational, and theoretical research on Indian languages (ILs) to understand how ILs are processed in the mind, how they evolve, how they differ, how they converge, and how they are distributed structurally, and use these research insights to develop solutions for assessment of language proficiency, diagnosis and treatment of language disorders, second language learning and education, mental health and emotional assessment, and N LP and AI in low-resource Indian languages.

The Centre is led by a group of researchers from multiple disciplines (Cognitive Science, Linguistics, Computer Science, and Philosophy) at IIT Kanpur.

VISION & MISSION

Scientific understanding. Transformative innovation.

01

Vision statement

To position India as a global leader in language sciences by harnessing its unparalleled linguistic diversity, fostering cutting-edge interdisciplinary research, and driving transformative innovations in health, technology, education, culture, and industry.

02

Mission statement

The Centre aims to advance the scientific understanding of Indian languages and develop innovative applications for health, technology, and education. By uniting experts in linguistics, psycholinguistics, cognitive science, neuroscience, computer science, AI engineering, speech-language pathology, epidemiology, education psychology, and data science, the Centre will create open resources, scalable technologies, diagnostic tools, and educational solutions.

This multidisciplinary effort, with an emphasis on deeper theoretical understanding of Indian languages, will accelerate discoveries in language processing, enable AI technologies in native languages, enhance healthcare outcomes, and preserve India’s linguistic heritage.
