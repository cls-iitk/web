Projects

Research projects

A1

Multilingual word corpus development

Investigator: Ark Verma

We are developing large-scale word corpora for multiple Indian languages containing psycholinguistically important information about a word, such as frequency, contextual diversity, word length, familiarity, and age of acquisition, among others.

A2

Benchmark experimental data for proficiency assessment in multiple Indian languages

Investigators: Ark Verma, N.P. Sudharshan, Chaithra Puttuswamy

We are conducting large-sample experimental studies on children and adult to collect data on reading, writing, speaking, comprehension proficiency in twelve Indian languages. We plan to build a large-scale multilingual benchmark for proficiency assessment in Indian languages.

A3

Development of standardized proficiency assessment tests for Indian languages

Investigators: N.P. Sudharshana, Ark Verma, Lalit Saraswat

We will develop a multiple standardized proficiency assessment battery for Indian languages. The assessment battery will be tested for its validity, reliability, and robustness.
