Vision & Mission

Scientific understanding.
Transformative innovation.

01
Vision

To advance the scientific understanding of language through interdisciplinary research and translate this knowledge into transformative applications for society.

02
Mission

The Centre aims to advance the scientific understanding of language and develop innovative applications for health, technology, education, and culture.

By bringing together expertise in linguistics, psycholinguistics, cognitive science, neuroscience, computer science, AI engineering, speech-language pathology, education, and data science, the Centre seeks to create scientific resources, scalable technologies, diagnostic tools, and educational solutions.
